# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Jilva a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience level,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

Positive: empathy, respectful feedback, grace for mistakes, focus on what is best for the community.
Unacceptable: sexualized language, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` contact or open a private Discussion).
Maintainers will review, warn on first minor offense, and temporarily or permanently ban for serious or repeated violations.
